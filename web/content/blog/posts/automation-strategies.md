# Advanced Automation Strategies

Automation is key to staying ahead in the fast-paced Solana ecosystem. In this guide, we'll cover how to leverage Solana Bots to create advanced trading strategies.

## The Volume Bot

The built-in volume bot allows you to simulate organic activity across multiple wallets, helping to bootstrap liquidity and build momentum for new tokens. 

### Setting Up Your First Strategy
1. Navigate to the **Automation** tab.
2. Select your target token pair.
3. Configure your delay between trades and total volume targets.
4. Execute!

Happy trading!
