# Welcome to Solana Bots

We are thrilled to announce the launch of Solana Bots! Our goal is to provide traders with the ultimate toolset for managing multiple wallets and executing complex trading strategies on the Solana blockchain.

## Why Solana Bots?

Managing multiple wallets and executing mass token swaps can be a tedious and error-prone process. We built Solana Bots to simplify this workflow while prioritizing security and performance.

### Key Features
*   **Self-Custodial:** Your keys never leave your device.
*   **Mass Swaps:** Execute trades across dozens of wallets simultaneously.
*   **Volume Bot:** Automate your trading strategies with our powerful built-in volume bot.
