# Security Best Practices for Self-Custody

When using tools like Solana Bots, it's essential to understand that you are solely responsible for your private keys. Self-custody means absolute control, but it also means no centralized entity can recover your assets if things go wrong.

## Tips for Staying Safe

1. **Never Share Private Keys:** Your private key should never leave your device unencrypted.
2. **Use Hardware Wallets:** Where possible, leverage hardware wallets for long-term storage and only use "hot" wallets for active trading sessions.
3. **Verify Downloads:** Always ensure you are downloading Solana Bots directly from our official sources and verifying checksums if available.

Trade safely!
